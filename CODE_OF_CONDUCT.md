# Code of Conduct

CofferHouse is community first. Participation must remain respectful, constructive and safe.

## Expected behavior

- communicate with respect;
- give actionable feedback;
- document assumptions and limitations;
- welcome participants with different experience levels;
- protect private and security-sensitive information;
- disclose conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, discrimination or personal attacks;
- threats or intimidation;
- spam or deceptive promotion;
- knowingly false claims about products, audits or returns;
- publishing another person's private information;
- exploiting vulnerabilities instead of reporting them responsibly.

Reports may be sent to `thecofferhouse@gmail.com`.
