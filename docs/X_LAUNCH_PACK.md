## Launch thread

### 1/6

CofferHouse Agent Suite is now public.

It is not a trading bot. It is an inspectable research system built on Arc: observe markets, apply visible rules, compare evidence, request one human decision and preserve a verifiable trail.

### 2/6

The room has 8 bounded agents:

Scout → Opportunity → Strategy → DEX → Action Center → Guardian → Automation → Interop Observer.

Each has one job. Their permissions do not silently expand as evidence moves through the system.

### 3/6

Scout reads supported Arc markets, applies versioned policy profiles and explains the first condition that needs attention.

Opportunity and Strategy then turn the same evidence into a shortlist and a capped, reserve-aware research proposal.

### 4/6

DEX research starts from a contract watchlist, not a ticker guess. Action Center records one expiring human approval. Guardian compares the approved intent with later observations. Automation only proves whether a hypothetical request fits revocable limits.

### 5/6

Interop Observer adds crosschain evidence: it reads Arc CCTP V2 events, de-duplicates transaction logs, compares observation windows and seals a receipt.

This is observation and verification — not a bridge, signer or relayer.

### 6/6

Every important step produces evidence that can be downloaded and independently checked. Change a sealed field and verification fails.

The House is playful. The financial boundaries are not.

## Standalone build-in-public posts

### Product boundary

Autonomy does not have to mean unlimited execution.

Our Agent Suite can observe, compare, rank, remember and escalate. It cannot custody funds, sign messages or send transactions.

We are proving the research and control layers first.

### Receipt verification

A CofferHouse receipt is a reproducible evidence package, not a certificate of profit.

It records inputs, policy version, result and integrity hash. If a sealed field changes, independent verification fails.

### Simulation

Before execution comes simulation.

The borrow preview shows the projected liquidity and utilization impact, labels the result clearly, and creates a separate downloadable receipt. No wallet. No signature. No transaction.

### Interoperability

Crosschain finance needs evidence, not just routes.

Interop Observer now reads Arc CCTP V2 events and compares non-overlapping windows so the Agent Suite can show what was observed without pretending to control the bridge.

### CofferHouse connection

The Agent Suite is not a separate brand. It is the working Agent Room inside CofferHouse — connected from the House and linked back to it.

One product. One visual language. Clear boundaries between research today and execution later.
